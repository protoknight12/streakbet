How to play Blackjack
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their value, J/Q/K are 10, an Ace is 11 or 1.
  • Hit takes a card, Stand stops, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card.
  • The dealer draws to 17. A win pays 2x your bet, a two-card 21 (blackjack) pays 2.5x, a tie returns your bet.

      How to play Belot
      • Two teams of two; your partner sits across. First team to 151 points wins ($600 prize, $40 for every hand your team takes).
      • Bidding: with 5 cards, choose a trump suit, No trumps or All trumps, or pass. Each bid must beat the last; three passes end it. You then get 3 more cards.
      • Playing: follow the suit that was led. If you can't, you must trump, unless your partner is already winning. Playable cards are lit up.
      • Card strength in trumps: J (20), 9 (14), A (11), 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Other suits: A (11), 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7.
      • Bonuses: sequences of 3/4/5+ cards (20/50/100), four of a kind (100-200), K+Q of trumps "Belot" (20), last trick (10), all 8 tricks (90). Declared for you automatically.
      • If the bidding team ends with fewer points than the other team, they get nothing ("вътре") and the defenders take everything.

      Свара

      Three cards each, one round of betting, best hand takes the pot. A tie is a свара: the pot stays, and everyone else can buy into the replay for half of it.

      How to play Свара
      • Everyone pays the ante and gets 3 cards. Then, starting left of the dealer: Пас (fold), Плащам (call; Чек when there's nothing to call) or Вдигам (raise).
      • When the bets are level, the hands still in are shown. A hand scores the total of its best suit: A 11, K Q J 10 count 10, 9 8 7 their number.
      • Two aces make 22. Three of a kind make three times the card (three aces 33). Three sevens are the best hand: 34.
      • The 7♣ is the шеха: it's wild and adds 11 to whichever suit is best.
      • A tie is a свара: the pot stays and the tied players play again. Anyone else at the table may join the replay by paying half the pot.
      • Table stakes: nobody can raise more than the shortest stack still in can call. Leaving cashes out your chips.
